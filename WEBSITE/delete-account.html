User Data Rights

Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and Global Data Protection Standards

Please Note Before Proceeding

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your business profile, customer records, invoices, item catalog, and stored assets cannot be recovered.

1. What Data Will Be Permanently Deleted?

Upon receiving and verifying your deletion request, we will completely purge:

  • User Account: Email, encrypted credentials, authentication sessions, and security tokens.
  • Business Profile: Business name, legal entity name, GSTIN, PAN, addresses, phone number, and bank/UPI configurations.
  • Customers & Ledgers: All customer directory entries, client contact details, and balance records.
  • Items Catalog: All product and service catalog entries, HSN/SAC codes, and custom pricing rules.
  • Invoices & Payments: All historical invoices, line items, payment records, and tax calculations.
  • Media Assets: All uploaded logos and saved digital signature files stored in our cloud buckets.

2. What Data May Be Temporarily Retained?

We retain zero operational data after account deletion. However, anonymized diagnostic server logs may be retained for up to 30 days strictly for security audit purposes, after which they are permanently wiped.

3. How to Request Account Deletion

You do not need the mobile app installed to delete your account. You can request deletion using either of the methods below:

Method A: Submit Deletion Request Online

Fill in your registered details below and click Submit to generate an authenticated deletion ticket.

✅ Deletion Request Initiated

Your email client has been prepared. Please send the pre-filled email to confirm your ownership. Our team will verify and execute the permanent purge within 48 to 72 hours.

Method B: Direct Email Request

Alternatively, send an email from your registered email address to:

📧 invoicify.app.support@gmail.com

Subject: Invoicify Account Deletion Request

4. Turnaround Time & Confirmation

Upon receipt of your request, our privacy team verifies ownership and completely purges all associated records within 48 to 72 business hours. You will receive a final confirmation email once the deletion is finalized.
