Core Capabilities

Everything You Need for Effortless Invoicing

Designed specifically for Indian sole proprietors, agencies, contractors, and merchants.

₹

Automated Indian GST Splits

Automatic calculation of Intra-State (CGST + SGST) vs Inter-State (IGST) taxes based on Place of Supply. Pre-configured GST tax slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and HSN/SAC codes.

📋

Non-GST & Bill of Supply Mode

Not registered under GST? One toggle switches to clean Bill of Supply, Proforma Invoice, or Estimate mode with zero tax clutter and custom document headers.

📱

Dynamic UPI QR Code Generator

Automatically generates scannable UPI QR codes directly on invoices. Clients scan via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM to pay the exact outstanding rupee amount.

👥

Client CRM & Live Ledgers

Store customer contact info, GSTINs, and billing addresses. Track lifetime sales, total payments, and outstanding balances with one-tap WhatsApp account statements.

✍️

Digital Signature & Brand Logos

Upload your company logo and draw legal authorized signatures directly on a finger-drawn canvas. Automatically compressed to guarantee fast rendering.

📊

Financial Reports & CSV Export

Review monthly revenue, collect GST slab summaries, and export RFC-4180 compliant CSV spreadsheets for seamless CA accounting and tax filing.

Bank-Grade Protection

Your Financial Data Belongs to You

Invoicify employs end-to-end TLS 1.3 transit encryption, PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) isolation, and encrypted device keychains. We do not sell your customer or transaction data to advertisers.
