Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Version 1.1

1. Introduction

Welcome to Invoicify ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and business financial information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data when you use the Invoicify mobile application and associated services (the "App").

By downloading, accessing, or using Invoicify, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate GST invoicing, financial records, client ledgers, and PDF generation, Invoicify collects the following categories of information:

A. Account & Business Profile Information

User email address, business legal name, trade name, GSTIN (Goods & Services Tax Identification Number), PAN (Permanent Account Number), registered business address, city, state, postal code, phone number, and bank account/UPI ID details used for payment remittance QR codes.

B. Customer & Client CRM Data

Contact person names, client company names, client phone numbers, emails, billing/shipping addresses, state codes, and client GSTINs entered by you to generate invoices.

C. Product, Catalog & Transaction Information

Product and service descriptions, HSN/SAC codes, quantities, unit prices, tax rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%), discounts, payment records, transaction reference IDs, and generated invoice history.

D. User Media Assets (Logos & Signatures)

Company logo images and finger-drawn digital signatures that you choose to upload or draw for embedding onto your PDF invoices. These are compressed to $\le$ 200 KB and stored securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • Creating, formatting, and generating GST-compliant and Non-GST PDF invoices.
  • Calculating automated CGST, SGST, and IGST tax splits and round-offs based on Indian tax laws.
  • Rendering dynamic UPI payment QR codes on invoices for instant scan-and-pay transactions.
  • Maintaining your client ledger, catalog records, and financial summary reports.
  • Syncing your business records across your devices via encrypted cloud databases.
  • Providing customer support and communicating critical account updates.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal, client, or financial information to data brokers or third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

We only share data with vetted infrastructure service providers necessary to operate our cloud functionality:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Cloud Storage): For secure user authentication, encrypted database storage, Row Level Security (RLS) policy enforcement, and asset hosting.
  • Google Fonts: For rendering high-quality typography in generated PDF documents.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and operational safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and our servers use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database employs granular PostgreSQL RLS rules ensuring each user can only read, write, or access their own authenticated records.
  • Secure Local Storage: Auth tokens and sensitive keys are stored using encrypted device keychains via FlutterSecureStorage.
  • Single-File Asset Quotas: Brand logos and signatures are strictly capped to $\le$ 200 KB and managed with single-file lifecycles to prevent data accumulation.

6. Account Deletion & User Rights

You retain complete control over your personal and business data. Under applicable data privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access, update, or edit any of your business or customer records directly in the app.
  • Export your invoice registers and financial ledgers as CSV spreadsheets anytime.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data, you can submit a request anytime through our Account Deletion Page or email us directly at invoicify.app.support@gmail.com. Requests are permanently executed within 48 to 72 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Invoicify is a business financial tool intended for entrepreneurs, contractors, and commercial enterprises. It is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our features or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
